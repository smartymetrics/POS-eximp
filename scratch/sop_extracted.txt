[Heading 3] 1.0 INTRODUCTION
============================================================

============================================================
[normal] 1.1 Purpose of the Manual
============================================================
This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) Manual is the ultimate governing document for Eximp & Cloves Infrastructure Ltd. It establishes the official operational, administrative, and strategic framework of the company. The primary purpose is to eliminate ambiguity, prevent operational silos, and ensure that every action taken by staff and management aligns with the company's strategic goals and regulatory obligations. It transitions the company from a "founder-driven" operation to a "process-driven" institution.

============================================================
[normal] 1.2 Objectives of the SOP
============================================================

============================================================
[normal] Standardisation: To ensure that every client experiences the same premium level of service, whether they interact with the Abuja or Lagos office.
============================================================

============================================================
[normal] Risk Mitigation: To protect the company from legal, financial, and reputational liabilities by enforcing strict compliance protocols.
============================================================

============================================================
[normal] Operational Efficiency: To eliminate redundant tasks and establish clear Service Level Agreements (SLAs) for interdepartmental requests.
============================================================

============================================================
[normal] Accountability: To provide a definitive framework for performance management, ensuring every task has a single point of ownership.
============================================================

============================================================
[normal] 1.3 Scope of the Manual
============================================================
The scope of this manual encompasses the entire lifecycle of Eximp & ClovesΓÇÖ operations. This includes executive governance, land acquisition protocols, human capital management, financial controls, marketing strategies, sales conversion workflows, legal compliance, and the Real Estate Bankers Club (REBC) administration.

============================================================
[normal] 1.4 Application of the Manual
============================================================
The policies and procedures detailed herein apply to all permanent employees, contract staff, executive management, board members, and affiliated realtors/partners operating under the Eximp & Cloves brand name. Ignorance of the manual is not a valid defense in disciplinary hearings.

============================================================
[normal] 1.5 Users of the Manual
============================================================

============================================================
[normal] Executive Management: Uses the manual to enforce governance and audit departmental performance.
============================================================

============================================================
[normal] Department Heads (HODs): Uses the manual to train staff, measure KPIs, and execute daily operations.
============================================================

============================================================
[normal] Staff & Trainees: Uses the manual as a daily reference guide for executing tasks and understanding workplace boundaries.
============================================================

============================================================
[normal] 1.6 Manual Review and Update Cycle
============================================================

============================================================
[normal] Annual Review: A mandatory comprehensive review will be conducted every November by the SOP Review Committee (comprising the CEO, Head of HR, Head of Legal, and Head of Operations).
============================================================

============================================================
[normal] Ad-Hoc Updates: Triggered by significant shifts in corporate strategy, new real estate legislation, or the launch of a fundamentally new product line.
============================================================

============================================================
[normal] Version Control: All updates will be logged in the Amendment Appendix (Section 11) and disseminated via official company email.

[Heading 3] 3.0 CORPORATE GOVERNANCE FRAMEWORK
============================================================

============================================================
[normal] 3.1 Governance Structure
============================================================
Eximp & Cloves operates a transparent, multi-tiered governance structure designed to separate oversight from day-to-day execution.

============================================================
[normal] The Board of Directors: Provides fiduciary oversight, approves annual budgets, and sets the macro-strategic vision of the company.
============================================================

============================================================
[normal] Executive Management Team: Led by the Managing Director/CEO, this team is responsible for translating the Board's vision into operational reality and driving company profitability.
============================================================

============================================================
[normal] 3.2 Leadership Framework
============================================================
Leadership at Eximp & Cloves is transformational and accountable. Leaders (Executives and HODs) are expected to:
Mentor subordinates.
Enforce SOPs without bias.
Take extreme ownership of their department's successes and failures.

============================================================
[normal] 3.3 Management Hierarchy & Chain of Command
============================================================
To prevent operational chaos, all staff must adhere to the formal chain of command:
Entry-Level / Officers Γ₧ö Team Leads Γ₧ö Department Heads (HODs) Γ₧ö Executive Management Γ₧ö Board of Directors.
Exception: "Line-jumping" is strictly prohibited unless it involves whistleblowing on fraud, sexual harassment, or gross operational sabotage by an immediate supervisor.

============================================================
[normal] 3.4 Decision-Making Authority
============================================================

============================================================
[normal] Strategic Decisions (Tier 1): Land acquisitions, new estate pricing, legal settlements, and capital expenditures above Γéª5,000,000 are strictly reserved for Executive Management.
============================================================

============================================================
[normal] Operational Decisions (Tier 2): Campaign approvals, departmental expense approvals up to Γéª500,000, and hiring recommendations are delegated to Department Heads (HODs).
============================================================

============================================================
[normal] Routine Execution (Tier 3): Standard client dispute resolution and daily task execution are empowered to Team Leads and Officers based on predefined SOP workflows.
============================================================

============================================================
[normal] 3.5 Internal Control System (Maker-Checker)
============================================================
To mitigate fraud and human error, Eximp & Cloves enforces a strict dual-control (Maker-Checker) system for all critical business processes. No single individual has the end-to-end authority to initiate and finalize a critical transaction.
The Maker: Initiates the request (e.g., Finance Officer prepares a vendor payment).
The Checker: Verifies accuracy, budget availability, and policy compliance (e.g., Head of Finance reviews).
The Approver: Authorizes the final action (e.g., CEO authorizes the bank transfer).

============================================================
[normal] 3.6 Approval and Escalation Matrix
============================================================
All operational blockages must be escalated based on the following timeline:

============================================================
[normal] Level 1 (Officer Level): If an issue cannot be resolved within 24 hours, it must be escalated to the Team Lead.
============================================================

============================================================
[normal] Level 2 (Team Lead Level): If unresolved within 48 hours, escalate to the HOD.
============================================================

============================================================
[normal] Level 3 (HOD Level): If the issue poses a financial loss, legal threat, or severe reputational damage, it must be escalated to the Executive Office immediately (within 2 hours of discovery).
============================================================
